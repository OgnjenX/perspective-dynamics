# EXP005 Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

The performance-optimal perspective dwell timescale increases with the evidence required to retain a frame.

## Confirmatory prediction

Across thresholds 0.0005, 0.001, 0.002, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, and 0.04, the optimal block length will exhibit:

- Spearman rank correlation ≥ 0.8 with ordered threshold;
- at most one adjacent downward reversal.

Ties in mean peak activation are resolved prospectively by selecting the shorter block. Peak activation remains the primary outcome.

## Disconfirmation

The scaling hypothesis is not confirmed if either criterion fails on held-out seeds. Alternate smoothing, excluding thresholds, changing tie rules, or selecting another outcome after inspection would be exploratory.

## Interpretation boundary

Confirmation would establish a reproducible calibration relation inside this controller: evidence demand constrains useful dwell. It would not establish universality, biological novelty, or emergent neural timing without replication across task structures and self-timed dynamics.
