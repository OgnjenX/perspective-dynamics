# Prospective criteria

H13.1: physical, functional, and goal predictive modules each achieve held-out
target accuracy at least 0.80.

H13.2: every pair of modules differs on at least 20% of episode-pair
co-clustering decisions, showing nonidentical organization.

H13.3: each module exceeds its matched majority-label baseline by at least 0.20.

H13.4: a cross-map table learned from joint category activations predicts the
held-out goal category from physical and functional categories with accuracy at
least 0.75.

Overall success requires H13.1–H13.4. Distinct category organization is treated
as a candidate computational perspective, not a biological perspective state.
