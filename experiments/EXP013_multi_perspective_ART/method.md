# Method

The same vectorized episodes are supplied to three predictive vigilance
learners. Only the supervised prediction target differs: physical transition,
functional consequence, or goal relevance. Category identifiers are emergent
within each module.

Held-out accuracy, category count, reset count, and pairwise co-clustering
distance are reported. A training-only cross-map associates pairs of physical
and functional categories with goal categories and is evaluated on held-out
objects.
