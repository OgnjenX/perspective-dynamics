# EXP006 Hypothesis

## Target finding

F001 states that the performance-optimal perspective dwell timescale increases with the evidence required to retain a frame in the current synthetic model.

## Positive-control criterion

For path topology with source-normalized propagation, the EXP005 criteria must replicate on unseen seeds:

- Spearman rank correlation ≥ 0.8 between ordered evidence thresholds and selected optimal block;
- at most one adjacent downward reversal.

Failure invalidates the experimental bridge before any novel condition is interpreted.

## Novel-environment robustness criteria

Each of the other three topology × propagation environments must independently satisfy all of:

1. Spearman rank correlation ≥ 0.6;
2. at most two adjacent downward reversals;
3. at least three distinct selected optimal blocks across seven thresholds;
4. at least four thresholds selecting an interior block from 2 through 40 steps rather than a grid boundary.

The generalization attempt passes only if all three novel environments pass. One failure is sufficient to retain F001 as model-specific and reject the stronger topology/propagation-robust claim.

## Interpretation boundaries

Passing would show robustness only within two related graph families and two deterministic linear propagation rules. It would not establish self-timed dynamics, learned perspectives, biological plausibility, human creativity, or universality.

Failure caused by ceiling, floor, or boundary optima remains a failure of the frozen generalization test. A later scale-calibrated experiment may diagnose it but cannot retroactively rescue this result.
