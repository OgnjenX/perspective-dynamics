# EXP002 Hypothesis

## Primary hypothesis

With all non-geometric factors controlled, the useful perspective will make the goal more accessible than the initial or irrelevant perspectives.

## Prospective predictions

1. `fixed_useful` will have a higher success rate and peak goal activation than `fixed_initial`.
2. `fixed_initial` will outperform `fixed_irrelevant`, reflecting their six- versus eight-edge cue-to-goal distance.
3. First passage will occur earlier when the goal is geometrically closer.
4. The useful-frame advantage will reproduce across the 50 prespecified seeds.

The `mixed` condition is a control for simultaneous availability of all relations. Its direction is not a primary prediction because blending can introduce shortcuts while diluting each frame’s edges.

## Disconfirmation

The primary hypothesis is not supported if the useful frame fails to improve continuous goal activation and thresholded accessibility, or if the difference can be traced to unequal nodes, edge count, weight distribution, cue input, compute, or direct goal cueing.

## Interpretation boundary

Support would establish a controlled representational-geometry effect and validate perspective matrices as an experimental manipulation. It would not show that a system can discover the useful perspective, that adaptive switching is superior, or that the task measures creativity.
