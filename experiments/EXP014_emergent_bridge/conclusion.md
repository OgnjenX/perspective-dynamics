# Conclusion

EXP014 failed its overall frozen conjunction because H14.2 failed. The
predictive learner selected the successful candidate in all five held-out
environments, inferred `elevated + stable + structure`, was identity-invariant,
and fell from 1.0 to 0.0 success when either elevation or stability was removed.
It beat exact-memory planning (0.0) and mean random clustering (0.016).

However, the prediction-free SOM also achieved 1.0 success. The bridge
conjunction was recoverable from similarity structure alone. Predictive
learning did not provide the required advantage over all controls.

This demonstrates transferable representation discovery without a supplied
bridge feature, but not a unique predictive mechanism, creativity, or biology.
