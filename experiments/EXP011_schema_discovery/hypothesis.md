# Prospective criteria

H11.1: competitive learning and SOM each achieve cluster purity at least 0.80
on training episodes.

H11.2: each achieves held-out outcome accuracy at least 0.80 on disjoint object
identities.

H11.3: identity scrambling leaves assignments and predictions unchanged.

H11.4: SOM outcome accuracy exceeds the mean random-clustering accuracy by at
least 0.20 over the frozen seeds.

Overall success requires H11.1–H11.4. The hand-written rule is an upper-bound
reference, not part of the conjunction.
