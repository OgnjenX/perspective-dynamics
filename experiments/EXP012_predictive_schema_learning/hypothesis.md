# Prospective criteria

H12.1: the predictive learner reaches held-out outcome accuracy at least 0.85.

H12.2: it exceeds SOM-without-prediction accuracy by at least 0.20 and mean
random-category accuracy by at least 0.25.

H12.3: at least one prediction mismatch causes category reset/refinement.

H12.4: identity scrambling leaves predictions unchanged.

Overall success requires H12.1–H12.4. Category count is not required to match
SOM because adaptive category creation is part of the tested mechanism; model
size is reported as a cost.
