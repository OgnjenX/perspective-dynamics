# Literature Map

## Purpose and limits

This is a Week 1 orientation map, not a systematic review. Searches were used to identify foundational and recent peer-reviewed work spanning creativity-network dynamics, insight/restructuring, cognitive flexibility, and representational geometry. Citation counts below are snapshot metadata from Consensus on 2026-08-13 and will change. Claims in this project must not outrun the methods of the cited studies.

## 1. Dynamic network accounts of creative cognition

Creative idea production has been associated with cooperation among default, executive-control, and salience systems. In a divergent-thinking fMRI study, early default–salience coupling was followed by later default–executive coupling, motivating a temporally structured rather than single-network account [1]. Whole-brain connectivity spanning these systems also predicted individual creative ability across internal and external validation samples [2].

Recent evidence makes the switching hypothesis more specific. Across ten samples (N = 2,433), DMN–ECN switching predicted divergent-thinking ability, with an inverted-U relation for switching balance; a separate task-fMRI sample provided converging evidence [3]. Generation and evaluation also show distinguishable dynamic patterns, warning against treating creativity as one homogeneous state [4].

**Implication:** the project should model coordinated generation, switching, and evaluation. Network correlation does not establish that perspective change is the mechanism, so the theory must make additional geometry-specific predictions.

## 2. Insight and representational change

Representational Change Theory proposed that impasses can be broken through constraint relaxation and perceptual chunk decomposition; four matchstick-arithmetic experiments supported differentiated predictions from these mechanisms [5]. A recent review distinguishes restructuring from the subjective Aha! experience and emphasizes that they can dissociate [6]. A 2025 fMRI study linked successful visual insight with stronger cortical representational change and hippocampal/amygdala activity, with representational change and hippocampal effects predicting later memory [7].

**Implication:** do not use self-reported insight as a proxy for perspective change. Independently measure the hypothesized representation and its temporal change.

## 3. Cognitive flexibility

Cognitive and behavioral flexibility depends on distributed lateral/orbital frontoparietal, midcingulo-insular, and frontostriatal systems across human and animal evidence [8].

**Implication:** generic flexibility is a strong alternative explanation. Experiments need matched task-switching and perturbation controls to isolate perspective-specific transformation.

## 4. Representational geometry

Population-level representational geometry links multivariate response patterns to decodability, information, and behavior, while remaining compatible with multiple underlying tuning schemes [9].

**Implication:** geometry supplies quantitative language, not automatic mechanism. Metrics should be preregistered, cross-validated, and connected to behavior or intervention.

## 5. Dynamical systems and biological grounding: open map

Topics for the next review pass:

- neural manifolds, contextual computation, mixed selectivity, and subspace gating;
- attractors, metastability, bifurcations, and non-equilibrium cortical dynamics;
- hippocampal relational inference and episodic simulation;
- neuromodulatory and frontostriatal control of switching;
- Grossberg ART/SMART mechanisms: attention, competition, vigilance, mismatch, and reset;
- spatial and abstract reference-frame transformations;
- formal creativity models, semantic search, and novelty/usefulness measurement.

These categories are intentionally marked open; Week 1 does not claim a complete synthesis.

## 6. Central gap

Current work supports dynamic network interaction, cognitive restructuring, and geometry-sensitive population analysis, but does not yet establish the full causal chain proposed here:

\[
\text{perspective control}
\rightarrow
\text{representational transformation}
\rightarrow
\text{changed state accessibility}
\rightarrow
\text{novel and useful solution}.
\]

The project’s contribution would be to formalize and test this chain against search, noise, information, task-switching, and readout-only explanations.
