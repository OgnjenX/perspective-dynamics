# Research milestones

Milestones mark changes in evidential status. They do not retroactively alter
the interpretation of earlier experiments.

## Level 1 Computational Principle Established

**Status:** Prototype only. Not yet evidence for creativity.

EXP008 demonstrates that an explicit relational engine can compose effects and
prerequisites across perspective-indexed schemas to produce a goal-directed
trajectory. The result establishes implementability and traceable provenance
in one hand-designed synthetic world.

It does not establish abstraction across object identities, learned schemas,
creative emergence, an advantage over ordinary planning, or biological
plausibility. Those questions remain open.

## Testing Genuine Compositional Emergence

**Status:** Next research milestone; experiments not yet completed.

EXP009 will test relational generalization across held-out objects and
environments with matched single-perspective, pooling, graph-search, random,
and name-scrambling controls. EXP010 will test whether integration creates a
trajectory absent from each individual perspective and from a static union
without the integration operation.

Biological interpretation remains gated on convincing computational evidence
from these experiments.
